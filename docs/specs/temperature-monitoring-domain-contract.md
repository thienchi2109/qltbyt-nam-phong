# Temperature Monitoring domain contract (TM-01)

Status: discovery contract for review; no runtime or database implementation is included.

This contract makes the decisions in [`temperature-monitoring.md`](./temperature-monitoring.md) explicit enough for the next tasks to implement. It keeps temperature monitoring inside Equipment and keeps its domain history separate from the existing equipment lifecycle timeline.

## Invariants

1. **Scope:** every read and write is constrained by the existing tenant (`don_vi`/facility) and department (`khoa_phong`) boundary. A global/admin role is recognized through `isGlobalRole()` outside the proxy; never compare only to `"global"`.
2. **Time:** persist event timestamps in UTC. Every effective configuration version has a non-null display timezone, defaulting to `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`; derive `monitoring_date` from the shift start in that version's timezone. Overnight shifts stay on their start date.
3. **Configuration history:** versions for one equipment cannot overlap in effective time. A reading snapshots lower/upper limits and relevant configuration fields at capture time; later configuration edits cannot reinterpret old data.
4. **Slot uniqueness:** the database enforces at most one canonical reading for `(equipment_id, monitoring_date, shift_id)`. Client double-click prevention is only a convenience.
5. **Reading validity:** a reading contains either a numeric °C value or a structured unable-to-measure reason; numeric values are evaluated against the captured inclusive limits. Units are °C in this release.
6. **Confirmation immutability:** a confirmed reading cannot be directly updated or deleted. Corrections use an amendment row and preserve the original plus its audit trail.
7. **Eligibility:** an equipment without an enabled effective configuration has no expected slots, cannot receive a monitoring reading, and is visibly marked as not configured. It is excluded from slot/status results unless an explicit administrative filter requests unconfigured equipment.
8. **Deviation integrity:** each threshold breach opens its own deviation with severity exactly from `Nhẹ`/`Nghiêm trọng`/`Khẩn cấp`. Closing requires both an immediate action and a verification result; a repair link is optional and does not merge lifecycles. Severity thresholds, escalation and reassignment are policy inputs for TM-06, not UI rules.
9. **Notification boundary:** v1 review notifications are in-app badge/inbox counts/items plus audit events. Web Push and continuous sensor alerts are deferred and cannot be assumed by a caller.
10. **Audit fail-closed:** a state-changing operation is rejected if its required business audit event cannot be recorded. Technical/security logs remain separate.
11. **Query boundary:** interactive queries default to 30 days, support 7/30/90-day presets and custom ranges up to 90 days, use server-side filters and cursor pagination, and never fetch outside the requested authorized window.
12. **Cache locality:** query keys include equipment, date range, shift and status (plus tenant/department scope where applicable). A mutation invalidates only related temperature-monitoring keys and preserves active filters.
13. **Presentation truthfulness:** charts expose raw points, captured threshold snapshots and deviation markers; aggregation must be labeled and cannot hide abnormal points. A keyboard/screen-reader table view is mandatory.
14. **Manual-monitoring boundary:** readings describe a manual observation at capture time; this module is not continuous sensor/IoT monitoring and must say so in the UI.

## State model and transitions

The slot state is exactly one of `Chưa ghi`, `Đã ghi`, `Bất thường` or `Chờ xác nhận`. It is a projection over the reading and amendment review; deviation has a separate lifecycle and never adds a `Closed` slot state. `Chưa ghi` has no canonical reading; the other states have one.

| Current                   | Event / guard                                | Next                              | Required side effects                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chưa ghi                  | Valid numeric reading within snapshot limits | Đã ghi                            | Insert canonical reading and audit event.                                                           |
| Chưa ghi                  | Unable-to-measure reason supplied            | Chờ xác nhận                      | Insert reading with reason; require reviewer decision.                                              |
| Chờ xác nhận              | Confirm unable-to-measure result             | Đã ghi                            | Preserve NULL value/reason and explicit unable flag; never count as measured/in-range numeric data. |
| Chưa ghi                  | Numeric reading outside limits               | Bất thường → Chờ xác nhận         | Insert reading, open one deviation, audit both; reviewer queue is visible.                          |
| Đã ghi                    | Submit amendment                             | Chờ xác nhận                      | Insert immutable amendment proposal; original remains effective until decision.                     |
| Bất thường / Chờ xác nhận | Approve amendment or review result           | Recompute as Đã ghi or Bất thường | Record decision and audit; deviation remains/updates according to resulting value.                  |
| Bất thường / Chờ xác nhận | Reject amendment                             | Prior effective state             | Preserve proposal and rejection reason; no overwrite.                                               |
| Đã ghi / Bất thường       | Authorized reopen with mandatory reason      | Chờ xác nhận                      | Append reopen audit event; direct edit remains forbidden.                                           |

Deviation is a separate state machine: `Mở` → `Đang xử lý` → `Chờ xác minh` → `Đã đóng`, or `Mở`/`Đang xử lý` → `Đã hủy` only with an authorized reason. Closing requires immediate action and verification result. Closing a deviation does **not** transition the slot to `Closed`: the slot remains `Bất thường` when its confirmed reading is out of range, or `Đã ghi` when an approved amendment/review recomputes it in range; an unresolved amendment remains `Chờ xác nhận`.

Conflict behavior is deterministic: a concurrent insert for the same slot returns a typed, recoverable conflict (HTTP/RPC conflict semantics), and the UI refetches that slot before offering amendment. Concurrent amendment/confirmation uses row locking or equivalent compare-and-swap; stale versions are rejected rather than silently merged.

## Immutable and versioned fields

- **Reading immutable after confirmation:** equipment id, monitoring date, shift id, captured-at UTC, actor, value/reason, notes, unit, lower/upper snapshot, configuration-version id, and original audit event.
- **Amendment immutable:** original reading id, proposed value/reason, mandatory rationale, author/time, reviewer decision/time, and resulting audit links. A rejected amendment is retained.
- **Deviation immutable facts:** source reading id, breach observation and opened-at/actor. Workflow fields may advance only through guarded transitions; closure evidence is retained.
- **Configuration history:** effective start/end, limits, schedule, display timezone, responsible person and method are versioned. Create a successor instead of changing existing policy fields. Owner-approved exception (2026-10-18): the manager RPC may close only the upper bound of an open-ended predecessor exactly at the successor's effective start, atomically with successor creation and a mandatory reason/actor audit. Preserve the predecessor's lower bound and every policy field; deletion remains forbidden. Reject retroactive changes and enforce the existing date/shift-boundary rule in the equipment timezone. A slot retains its slot-start policy through its full interval, including overnight and later backfill; reading snapshots remain unchanged. Failure rolls back the closure, successor and audit together.
